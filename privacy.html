Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Who we are

StockDesk Inventory ("StockDesk", "we", "us") makes point of sale and stock control software for shops. We operate from Uganda. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and shop name when you sign up.
  • Shop data you enter: products, prices, stock levels, sales, customers, suppliers, expenses and staff accounts.
  • Usage data: device type, browser and basic logs that help us keep the service working and secure.
  • Payment references for your StockDesk plan, such as Mobile Money transaction IDs. We never ask for your Mobile Money PIN.

How we use it

  • To run your till, stock, reports and staff features.
  • To bill your plan and confirm payments.
  • To help you on WhatsApp, by email or in the app.
  • To keep the service reliable and secure, and to improve it.

We do not sell your shop data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Customer contact details

When you save a customer's phone or WhatsApp number, you are responsible for having their permission. StockDesk uses those numbers only when you choose to send a receipt or reminder.

Who can see your data

Only the people you add to your shop can see its data, and only what their role allows. Our team may access an account to fix a problem you report or to protect the service.

Storage and security

Data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure with access controls, encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No online service is completely free of risk, so keep staff passwords private and remove access when someone leaves your shop.

How long we keep it

We keep your shop data while your account exists. If you ask us to close your account, we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable time, unless the law requires us to keep some records longer, such as billing history.

Your choices

  • Update shop, staff and customer details inside the app.
  • Export your products, customers and sales to Excel at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for your account to be deleted by contacting us.
